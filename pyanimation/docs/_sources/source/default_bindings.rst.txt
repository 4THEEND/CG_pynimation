
Default bindings
================

Here is the list of the default bindings of the :class:`~pynimation.viewer.viewer.Viewer`



+-------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Event                               | Description                                                                                                    |
+=====================================+================================================================================================================+
| Quit event, q, escape               | Exit PyNimation                                                                                                |
+-------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| s                                   | Capture a screenshot of the viewport                                                                           |
+-------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| right                               | Move right                                                                                                     |
+-------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| up                                  | Move forward                                                                                                   |
+-------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| down                                | Move backward                                                                                                  |
+-------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| r                                   | Toggle recording                                                                                               |
+-------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| left click, move mouse              | Move Camera                                                                                                    |
+-------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| page up                             | Move up                                                                                                        |
+-------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| left                                | Move left                                                                                                      |
+-------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| page down                           | Move down                                                                                                      |
+-------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
